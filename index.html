How it works

Walk. Decide. Watch. Learn.

step 01
Walk the yard
WASD and mouse, first person. A beacon marks the next checkpoint. Trucks loop, the RTG sways, gulls circle the quay.

        
step 02
Make the call
Three options fill the screen. One is right, two are what people actually do when they are late. Press A, B or C.

        
step 03

The kernel

        
The replay is the lesson.

        
A quiz tells you that you were wrong. A replay shows you the 25-tonne tractor, the 20 metres it needs to stop, and the three seconds you had. The regulation comes after the body, because that is the order in which it was written.

        
    
          
  • Scripted, not simulated. Each replay is a five-second scene with a fixed camera and timed captions. It reads the same every time, which is what a lesson needs.
    • 
          
  • Real rules, paraphrased. Every checkpoint cites the Indonesian regulation it comes from. The one-line summaries are ours and are flagged for practitioner review.
    • 
          
  • English first. Every screen ships in English. The sim has a one-tap switch to Bahasa Indonesia for the workers it is made for.

Why not VR
Headsets train the people who already have a training room.

Molca, Immerse, SimLab and others do serious VR work for ports and energy. ShiftReplay is the version for everyone who does not get a headset.
