The problem

Indonesia recorded 462,241 workplace accidents in 2024.

Two years earlier it was 298,137. At the ports, the accidents look like the ones in this sim: a box coming down, a vehicle and a person in the same lane.

Akurat

Who it is for. The new checker, driver or lasher on day one, and the HSE officer who has to induct them. Their induction today is usually a slideshow and a signature; VR patrols exist, but only where there is a headset and a training room.

Why a replay. A 2024 meta-analysis of 52 studies found that immersive safety training beat traditional training on both knowledge and retention (Scorgie et al., Safety Science 171). ShiftReplay tests how much of that a browser can carry without the headset.

How it works

From the gate to the shift report.

step 01
Pick a shift
Walk the yard with WASD and mouse, or take the controls: tractor, RTG crane, signaller, inspector, gas leak. A beacon marks the next checkpoint.

        
step 02
Make the call
Three options fill the screen. One is right, two are what people actually do when they are late. Press A, B or C.

        
step 03
Watch the replay
Wrong call: the camera cuts to third person and plays the accident in slow motion. In the hands-on modes it replays your own run.

        
step 04
Do the safe thing
Hold E to put on PPE or clip a harness. Hit SPACE the moment the driver waves. Then the shift report.

      

    


    


    

      

        
The kernel

        
The replay is the lesson.

        
A quiz tells you that you were wrong. A replay shows you the 25-tonne tractor, the 20 metres it needs to stop, and the three seconds you had. The regulation comes after the body, because that is the order in which it was written.

        
    
          
  • Your run, not a cutscene. At the controls, the sim records the last seconds of what you did and plays them back at half speed, with your speed, sway or gas reading on screen. Then it shows what happens next.
    • 
          
  • Scripted where it should be. On foot, each replay is a five-second scene with a fixed camera and timed captions. It reads the same every time, which is what a lesson needs.
    • 
          
  • Real rules, paraphrased. Every checkpoint cites the Indonesian regulation it comes from. The one-line summaries are ours and are flagged for practitioner review.
    • 
          
  • English first. Every screen ships in English. The sim has a one-tap switch to Bahasa Indonesia for the workers it is made for.
    •

Why not VR
Headsets train the people who already have a training room.

      
vr vs shiftreplay

      

        
VR safety trainingShiftReplay

        
          
HardwareHeadset per seat, PC, vendorAny laptop with a browser

          
ControlsHand controllersKeyboard and mouse: drive, lift, signal

          
Time to first lessonProcurement, install, onboardingOpen a link, click a role

          
Who sees itStaff at HQ with the roomThe contractor on day one

          
Adding a scenarioVendor projectOne JSON object, or one mode file

          
PresenceHigherLower. The replay makes up the difference

        
      

      

      
Molca, Immerse, SimLab and others do serious VR work for ports and energy. ShiftReplay is the version for everyone who does not get a headset.
