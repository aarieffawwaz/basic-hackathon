◼ HOW IT WORKS

Walk. Decide. Watch. Learn.

01

Walk the yard

WASD and mouse, first person. A beacon marks the next checkpoint. Trucks loop, the RTG sways, gulls circle the quay.

02

Make the call

Three options fill the screen. One is right, two are what people actually do when they are late. Press A, B or C.

03

Watch the replay

Wrong call: the camera cuts to third person and plays the accident in slow motion, with the physics in the captions.

04

Do the safe thing

Hold E to put on PPE or clip a harness. Hit SPACE the moment the driver waves. Then the shift report.

◼ THE KERNEL

The replay is the lesson.

A quiz tells you that you were wrong. A replay shows you the 25-tonne tractor, the 20 metres it needs to stop, and the three seconds you had. Regulations are introduced after the body, because that is the order in which they were written.

  • Scripted, not simulated. Each replay is a five-second scene with a fixed camera and timed captions. It reads the same every time, which is what a lesson needs.
  • Real rules, paraphrased. Every checkpoint cites the Indonesian regulation it comes from. The one-line summaries are ours and are flagged for practitioner review.
  • EN / ID. Judges read it in English; the people it is for read it in Bahasa Indonesia.
◼ THREE SHIFTS

Pick a job. Each one has different ways to go wrong.

Yard checker
On foot · 5 checkpoints

Gate PPE, the truck lane, the suspended load, the two-high stack, and whether you file the near-miss report.
Reach stacker operator
Heavy equipment · 3 checkpoints

Pre-start inspection on a warm machine, a checker in your blind spot, gusts at 14 m/s with a stack to build.
Lashing crew
Quay & vessel · 3 checkpoints

A gangway with no net, a stern line under 60 tonnes, and the fastest way to the top tier.
