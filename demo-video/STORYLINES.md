# ShiftReplay demo film — storylines

The one thing: a wrong call is answered with a slow-motion replay of the accident, not a red X.
The line to remember: "it showed me the accident before I made the mistake."
One viewer, one task: Dimas, 22, first shift as a yard checker, has to cross the truck lane.
The piece of data that travels through the film: the option "Cross now. 40 m is plenty."

Recommendation: A. It opens on the product doing the one thing, inside the real UI, and every later beat
is a real screen the viewer can go and click. B needs a staged "old way" that is not the product; C is the
bolder cut but asks the viewer to read a report before they know what a replay is.

## A. Forty metres (built, with B's opening folded in)

Device: a moment in someone's day, then the feature as the hook, then the shift. Form: object-to-object chain.
Revision: the film now opens on the problem, told in four lines over the product's own yard (Dimas at the gate,
the induction that was a slideshow and a signature, the tractor he has never stood next to), and the lane card
is the answer to "Today he does." Nothing staged: the First Shift card and gate are the sim, the lane shot is
the landing page's camera path.
The viewer sees the decision card already open at the truck lane, a big cursor pick the tempting answer,
and the tractor arrive in slow motion. Then the shift plays forward: pick a job, walk, choose again, wave,
three more replays, the shift report, the mark.

First two seconds: the real decision card fills the frame, "Truck lane: a terminal tractor is coming",
the cursor slides in from bottom-right toward option A.

hook (card, click A) → tension (replay, 32 km/h, 20 m, impact flash) → reveal (title "See the accident before it happens." + the near-miss card and the rule) → the name over the landing orbit → proof (pick a job · walk to the beacon · choose again · SPACE on the wave) → range (RTG drop, snap-back, fall) → payoff (shift report, the near-miss row) → claim (no headset, no account, no server) → end card (mark, wordmark, Start shift, URL)

Look: the footage is the ground; titles sit on the product's own dark glass panel, Inter 700, yellow #f5c518 on one word.
Sound: the sim's own port ambience (wind, diesel idle, gulls, a reverse beeper) carries the prologue alone; a cold minimal pulse at about 96 BPM enters as the lane card opens, drops to near silence under the first replay, comes back for the shift. A bass impact plus the sim's own thud on the flash, the sim's confirm tone on the safe call, clicks on real clicks, a whoosh on each scene seam, a riser into the mark.

## B. A slideshow and a signature

Device: life before and after. Form: split screen to one.
Left pane: an induction slide deck being clicked through and a form being signed, flat and silent. Right
pane: the same minute inside ShiftReplay, the tractor replay. The left pane shrinks and the right one
takes the frame.

First two seconds: a projector-grey slide "Keselamatan Kerja — Bab 3" and a pen signing a line.

hook (the slide) → tension (slide 14 of 60, signature) → reveal (the right pane: the replay) → proof (the shift) → payoff (report) → claim ("The replay is the lesson.") → end card

Look: the left pane desaturated office beige, the right pane the sim; type Inter. Sound: fluorescent hum and a clicker on the left, the music enters with the right pane.

## C. Rewound from the report

Device: the result first. Form: an endless zoom that runs backwards.
Open on the shift report, one row marked near miss. The camera dives into that row, the frame rewinds into
the near-miss card, then into the replay running backwards until the tractor is 40 m away and the card is
open again. Then it plays forward at full speed and ends on the mark.

First two seconds: the report, "4 / 5 clean calls", the camera already moving toward the red row.

hook (report row) → rewind (card, replay backwards) → reveal (the open card, the title) → proof (forward, fast) → payoff (the report again, now with the viewer knowing what the row cost) → end card

Look: the sim, with a timecode strip during the rewind. Sound: tape-rewind texture, then the bed.
