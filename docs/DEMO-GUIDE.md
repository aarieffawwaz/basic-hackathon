Tips
- Record at 1920×1080, browser zoom 100%, sound on (the thud matters).
- Press **Esc** before switching tabs; pointer lock swallows the cursor.
- Mute the ambience with **♪** if your mic picks it up and add it back in the edit.
- Nothing on screen should show credentials or personal folders.
